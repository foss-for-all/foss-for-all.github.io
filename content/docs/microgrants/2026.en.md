title: FOSS for All Microgrants 2026
---

2026 is the first year of the FOSS for All Microgrants pilot. We invited applications from free and open source projects and communities and announced five selected projects from 13 applicants on October 19, 2026. Applications are closed.

## Selected projects

### llvm-z80

An LLVM compiler backend targeting the Zilog Z80 processor.

[llvm-z80 repository](https://github.com/llvm-z80/llvm-z80)

### Spine

A backend framework that makes HTTP request handling and business logic execution explicit.

[Spine repository](https://github.com/NARUBROWN/spine)

### Deskband11Lib

A .NET library for displaying WinUI 3 or WPF widgets in the Windows 11 taskbar. It can be used to build always-visible tools such as small dashboards and media widgets.

[Deskband11Lib repository](https://github.com/airtaxi/Deskband11Lib)

### HornPenguin Booklet

Software that turns PDF files into booklets and signatures for binding. It helps users print folded sections of a book or pamphlets on a home printer.

[HornPenguin Booklet repository](https://github.com/HornPenguin/Booklet)

### Gukhanmun

A Rust/JavaScript library and tool that converts Korean documents written in a mixture of hanja and hangul into hangul-only text.

[Gukhanmun repository](https://github.com/dahlia/gukhanmun)

## Funding and payments

Funding is capped at KRW 200,000 per recipient. This is an expense limit, rather than a lump-sum payment made upon selection. We cover eligible expenses for the recipient's operations and activities through:

- Direct payments: FOSS for All pays vendors at the recipient's request.
- Reimbursements: FOSS for All reimburses expenses paid by a recipient's member after checking valid supporting documents.

Direct payments are preferred; costs that require automatic recurring payments, such as subscriptions, should be submitted for reimbursement. Recipients register on Open Collective, apply for FOSS for All to serve as their fiscal host, and submit expense requests through the platform. See the [recipient terms (in Korean)](https://forum.fossforall.org/t/foss-for-all-microgrants/164) for eligible expenses and documentation requirements, and the [Open Collective registration guide (in Korean)](https://forum.fossforall.org/t/open-collective-collective-fiscal-host-2026/148) for setup instructions.
